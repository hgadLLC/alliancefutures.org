Advisory Services

Commercial advisory work is conducted by Levenhall, a DC-based strategic advisory firm under common ownership with OTX International, TAFI's parent organization — with a clearly stated firewall to TAFI's public research.

TAFI is the public-research program. Levenhall is the commercial advisory firm. They share roots and certain personnel, and they are kept structurally and operationally separate so that neither compromises the other.

What Levenhall does

Levenhall is a Washington, DC–based strategic advisory firm focused on a specific problem: how international defense and security firms from allied democracies gain meaningful access to US government contracts. Levenhall does not broker introductions or sell market reports. It architects market entry — entity structure, regulatory pathway, procurement vehicle, and US-based leadership.

The work spans four lines:

  • Market Entry Advisory. Procurement landscape mapping, agency targeting, regulatory pathway analysis (ITAR / EAR / FAR / DFARS), and an operational entry plan.
  • Rapid Procurement Pathways. Architecting CSO, OTA, and follow-on production approaches that move from solicitation to award in 60–120 days.
  • Executive Placement. A vetted network of former senior government officials, military officers, and defense industry executives ready to lead US operations.
  • International Tax Services. Cross-border entity structuring, transfer pricing, and treaty analysis through Levenhall's partnership with CST Tax.

Who Levenhall serves

  • Defense and security firms based in the United Kingdom, Australia, Canada, New Zealand, NATO members, and select allied partners.
  • Allied governments and agencies seeking structured advisory on US procurement engagement.
  • Investors and primes evaluating allied defense technology entering the US market.

The firewall

How TAFI's public research stays independent of Levenhall's commercial work

Different vehicle. Commercial advisory engagements are contracted by Levenhall, not by TAFI. TAFI is a research program of OTX International, an independent 501(c)(3) public charity. Levenhall is a separate commercial firm.

No review rights. Levenhall clients have no review rights over TAFI's public research, briefings, or commentary — and TAFI publishes findings that cut against prior work as readily as findings that confirm it.

No briefing of drafts. Funders — commercial or philanthropic — do not see TAFI drafts before our peer reviewers do.

Disclosure. Where a TAFI publication touches on a topic on which Levenhall has done commercial work, we disclose the relationship in the piece.

Conflict checks. Levenhall conducts conflict and compliance review on each engagement before accepting it. Where a conflict cannot be cleanly managed, the engagement is declined.

Engagement

For advisory work — market entry, procurement strategy, or executive placement — reach Levenhall directly. For TAFI public-research inquiries, use the contact address on the homepage.
