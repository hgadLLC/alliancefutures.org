Support TAFI

Independent research on alliance futures is possible only with independent funding. If our work is useful, help us keep doing it.

TAFI is supported by foundation grants, individual donors, and commercial advisory work, with a strict firewall between commercial engagements and our public research. Every contribution goes into deeper analysis, more rigorous review, and sustained coverage of the regions that matter most.

Ways to Give

Individual Donors

Tax-deductible gifts of any size, made to OTX International, our fiscal parent.

Foundations

Programmatic and general operating support for multi-year research agendas.

Institutional Partners

Co-funded research, exercises, and convenings with universities, think tanks, and governments.

Our Firewall

No donor—individual, foundation, or institutional—sees drafts before our peer reviewers do. Commercial advisory work is conducted through a separate vehicle under separate terms. Commercial clients have no review rights over our public research or public remarks. We publish findings that cut against our prior work as readily as findings that confirm it.

Tax Status

OTX International is an independent, non-partisan 501(c)(3) public charity. Contributions are tax-deductible to the fullest extent permitted by law. A receipt will be provided for your records.
