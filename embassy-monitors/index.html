PRC Pacific Embassies Monitor

The PRC Pacific Embassies Monitor provides systematic, open-source tracking of Beijing’s public diplomatic activities across the nine Pacific Island Countries hosting Chinese missions. The monitor captures official embassy social media and website posts, supplemented by local sources, to offer weekly, structured intelligence reports that bridges critical information gaps on regional engagement.
