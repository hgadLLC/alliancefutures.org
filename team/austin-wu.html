Austin Wu

Email LinkedIn

Austin Wu is a Fellow at The Alliance Futures Initiative, where he conducts independent research on intelligence warfighting, Indo-Pacific deterrence, and the maritime industrial base.

Publications

His analysis has been published in outlets including The Hill, The Strategist, and Real Clear Defense.

Service

Austin also serves as an intelligence officer in the U.S. Army Reserve.
