Adam Leslie

President & Founder

A career spanning sovereign intelligence service, diplomatic engagement, military aviation, executive nonprofit leadership, and U.S. government market architecture — operating within the upper tier of independent national security strategists in Washington, DC.

LinkedIn

Adam Leslie is President and Founder of OTX International and The Alliance Futures Initiative. He is retained for judgment, structural insight, and disciplined execution in high-consequence environments.

Background

Adam is a former Australian military officer, diplomat and intelligence professional, with experience across national security, strategic policy, market entry, regional engagement and alliance development. His career spans sovereign intelligence service, diplomatic engagement, military aviation, executive nonprofit leadership, and U.S. government market architecture.

He previously served as Executive Director of ASPI USA, where he worked on Indo-Pacific security, defence industrial cooperation, AUKUS, technology policy and allied strategic integration.

Work at TAFI

Through TAFI, Adam focuses on converting allied strategic intent into practical policy, industrial and operational outcomes. His work examines the intersection of national security, economic statecraft, defence technology, irregular competition and regional resilience, with a particular focus on Australia’s role in the Indo-Pacific and the future of allied defence integration.
