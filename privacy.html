Privacy Policy

How TAFI handles information collected through this website.

Last updated: April 2026

The Alliance Futures Initiative (“TAFI,” “we”) is a research program of OTX International. We aim to collect as little information about visitors to this website as possible, and to use what we do collect only to operate, secure, and improve our work.

What we collect

This site is largely static and does not require accounts or registration. The categories of information that may be collected are limited to:

  • Server logs. Standard request data such as IP address, browser type, referring URL, and time of access, retained for security and diagnostic purposes.
  • Email correspondence. If you contact us at an address listed on this site, we will retain the contents of that exchange to respond and maintain a record of the inquiry.
  • Mailing list (if used). If you opt in to receive TAFI products by email, we collect the address you provide and the date of opt-in. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in any message.

What we do not collect

  • We do not sell or rent personal information.
  • We do not use third-party advertising trackers or behavioral profiling pixels.
  • We do not require accounts to read TAFI publications.

Cookies and analytics

TAFI may use a privacy-respecting analytics service to understand which pages are read and how visitors arrive. Where such a service is used, it is configured to avoid persistent cross-site tracking and to anonymize IP addresses. We do not use third-party advertising cookies.

External links

This site links to publications, articles, and tools hosted by third parties (publishers, social platforms, government sites, our parent organization OTX International). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those external services.

Children

TAFI does not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Your rights

If you are located in a jurisdiction with data-protection laws (e.g., the EU/UK GDPR, California's CCPA), you may have the right to request access to or deletion of any personal information we hold about you. To exercise these rights, write to contact@alliancefutures.org.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page when our practices change. The “Last updated” date at the top reflects the most recent revision. Material changes will be flagged in a brief notice on the homepage.

Contact

Questions about this policy or about TAFI's handling of your information can be sent to contact@alliancefutures.org.
